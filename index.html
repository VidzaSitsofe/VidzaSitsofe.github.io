About Me

Hello! I'm Vidza Sitsofe Grace, a passionate seamstress and textile artist dedicated to creating beautiful, handcrafted pieces. With years of experience in sewing and design, I transform quality Ghanaian fabrics into unique garments and accessories that tell a story.

My work combines traditional African sewing techniques with modern design aesthetics. Every stitch is made with care, and every project is a labor of love. Whether it's custom tailoring, unique apparel, or decorative textiles, I bring creativity and precision to every piece.

How to Measure

Accurate measurements = perfect fit

  1. Chest / Bust: Measure around the fullest part, keeping the tape parallel to the floor.
  2. Waist: Measure at the natural waistline (where your body bends).
  3. Hips: Measure around the fullest part of the hips.
  4. Shoulder Width: From shoulder tip to shoulder tip across the back.
  5. Arm Length: From the shoulder tip to the wrist with a slightly bent elbow.
  6. Neck: Around the base of the neck where a collar would sit.
  7. Length (Dress/Skirt): From natural waist to desired hem.

Tips: Measure over undergarments you plan to wear with the garment, keep the tape snug but not tight, and have someone help for best accuracy.

When you send measurements, include your height, typical clothing size, and any fit preferences (loose, fitted, high-waist, etc.).
